title:  What is a bribentive?
order: 5
---

A *bribentive* is a word we use in class for the small rewards handed out during
some activities. It is a blend of *bribe* and *incentive*: the reward is usually
just a sweet or a chocolate bar, so it is not really a good incentive, more a
small bribe for engagement. The games still work without it, but it adds a bit
of fun to the competition.
