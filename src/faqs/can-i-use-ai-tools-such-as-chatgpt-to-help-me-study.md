title: Can I use AI tools such as ChatGPT or Claude to help me study?
order: 3
---

You may, but I encourage you to be deliberate about how. The assessment for
this course is a closed-book examination with no generative AI allowed, so the
only thing that matters on the day is what you can do yourself.

The evidence so far is that these tools help or harm depending on how they are
used. In a
[field experiment with nearly a thousand mathematics students](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2422633122)
published in PNAS, students with unrestricted access to GPT-4 did much better
on practice problems, but once access was removed they did *worse* on the exam
than students who had never used it. Notably, a version of the tutor
constrained to give hints rather than answers removed most of that harm.

So if you use an LLM, use it in ways that keep you doing the thinking: ask it
to quiz you on the definitions, ask it for a hint rather than a solution, or
show it an attempt you have already written and ask it to find the error. Do
not ask it to answer an example question before you have attempted the
question in full yourself. Otherwise you are practising for an examination
that the AI sits, and not the one that you will.
