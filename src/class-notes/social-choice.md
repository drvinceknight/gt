## Activity (20 minutes)

**Goal.** Show that different voting rules can give different winners from the
same preferences. This doubles as a revision-topic vote.

List all topics from the course on Menti and ask students to choose which one we
revise in class.

Now list them all on <https://stablevoting.org> and discuss whether the approach
gives a different result.

## Discussion (20 minutes)

Work through the Social Choice chapter.

Discussion Point: **After the definition of Majority rule ask how these apply to
the revision topic choice.**

Discussion Point: **After the condorcet method and the borda method definitions
ask which one they feel was used by stablevoting.org?**

## From the activity to the exam answer

The activity above is written up as a marked exam question: **Question 1 (the in-class activity)** on the [Social Choice](/topics/social-choice.html) page, with a full worked solution. Closing the loop here is the step that helps students who find exams hard: work through that question together, or set it as the immediate follow-up, so they see the game they just played turned into a full-mark answer.
