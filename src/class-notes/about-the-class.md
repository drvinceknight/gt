**Duration**: 50 minutes

## Objectives

- Outline timetable and approach to class.
- Discuss feedback from previous years.
- Make students aware of learning resources available to them.
- Make students aware of assessment criteria.
- Make students aware of the coding aspect of the course (both in assessment and
  in content).
- Introduce games.

## Activity (20 minutes)

**Goal.** Open with a memorable game that exposes escalation and sunk cost,
and set the rhythm of activity-then-discussion for the course.

Play the dollar auction game (with £5).

> Top two bidders pay, winner gets to keep the £5.

Talk about strategy (briefly!), detail that we will do things like this every
class: first 20 minutes will be an activity and the second will be a discussion
of theory. Sometimes we will not finish the discussion.

**A note on bribentives.** Several later activities reward players with a
*bribentive*. The word is a blend of *bribe* and *incentive*: the reward is
usually just a sweet or a chocolate bar, so it is not really a good incentive,
more a small bribe for engagement.
