# Feedback

Part way through the module, and again at the end of it, I give over part of a
class to anonymous feedback: what is working, what is not, and anything else you
want to tell me. Every round is kept on this page, newest first, exactly as it
was written and with my response to it, so the record builds up year on year.

I publish it for two reasons. The first is that you can see the feedback is read
and acted on rather than collected and filed. The second is that the comments
tell you what the people who sat in your seat found hard, which is useful when
you are working out how to study.

Responses appear as they were submitted, including the typos. A few are cut
short because the polling tool limits how much can be typed into one answer.
