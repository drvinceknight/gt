\slidehead{Why they disagree}
\notesref{17.2.2}{Social Choice: Definition: A Social Welfare Function}

\vspace{0.9cm}

{\large Counting first choices looks only at
\answerrule[5cm]{the top of a ballot}, and throws away
\answerrule[5cm]{all the rest of it}.}

\vspace{1.1cm}

{\large Borda uses \answerrule[6cm]{the whole ranking}, so a topic that is
everybody's second choice can beat one that half the room ranks last.}

\vspace{1.1cm}

{\large A Condorcet winner beats every other topic
\answerrule[4.5cm]{head to head}, and sometimes
\answerrule[4cm]{there is none}.}
