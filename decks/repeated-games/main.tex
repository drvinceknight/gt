\slidehead{Grudger, against Grudger}
\notesref{7.2.2}{Repeated Games: Definition: Strategies in a repeated game}

\vspace{0.3cm}

{\large Grudger plays High until you play Low, then Low for ever. Always Low
plays Low every round.}

\vspace{0.6cm}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\linewidth}
{\large\bfseries You conform, and play High for ever}\\[0.5em]
{\large \(3 + 3\delta + 3\delta^{2} + \cdots \;=\;\)
\answerblank[3]{\(\dfrac{3}{1 - \delta}\)}}

\vspace{0.8cm}

{\large\bfseries You deviate to Low in round one}\\[0.5em]
{\large \(5 + \delta + \delta^{2} + \cdots \;=\;\)
\answerblank[3]{\(5 + \dfrac{\delta}{1 - \delta}\)}}
\end{minipage}

\newpage
\slidehead{How patient do we have to be?}
\notesref{7.2.7}{Repeated Games: Definition of individually rational payoffs}

\vspace{1.2cm}

\begin{center}
{\Large \(\dfrac{3}{1 - \delta} \;\ge\; 5 + \dfrac{\delta}{1 - \delta}
 \qquad \Longleftrightarrow \qquad \delta \;\ge\;\)
 \answerblank[2]{\(1/2\)}}
\end{center}

\vspace{1.6cm}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\linewidth}
\raggedright
{\large Our first die gives \(\delta =\)
\answerblank[1.8]{\(5/6\)}}\\[0.9em]
\answerspace{10.6}{4.0}{%
  \(5/6 \ge 1/2\), so cooperation holds: conforming is worth \(18\)
  against \(10\) for deviating.}
\end{minipage}
\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\linewidth}
\raggedright
{\large The second gives \(\delta =\)
\answerblank[1.8]{\(1/3\)}}\\[0.9em]
\answerspace{10.6}{4.0}{%
  \(1/3 < 1/2\), so it does not: conforming is worth \(9/2\) against
  \(11/2\) for deviating.}
\end{minipage}

\newpage
\slidehead{What if we knew it was the last round?}
\notesref{7.2.3}{Repeated Games: Subgame perfection of sequence of stage Nash profiles}

\vspace{1.4cm}

{\Large In a round you know is the last, the future is worth
\answerblank[1.6]{\(0\)}, so you bid \answerblank[2.4]{Low}.}

\vspace{1.6cm}

{\Large Both of you know that, so keep working backwards and cooperation
\answerrule[5cm]{unravels}.}

\vspace{1.6cm}

{\Large The die was doing real work: not by making the game long, but by
making the end \answerrule[5cm]{uncertain}.}
